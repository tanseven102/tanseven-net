Privacy Policy — Tan Seven Analytics

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Tan Seven Analytics is an internal tool used by Tan Seven to read performance statistics of YouTube channels that Tan Seven owns and manages. It is not offered to the public.

What data we access

With your permission through Google sign-in, the tool requests read-only access to:

  • YouTube Analytics data for your channel (views, watch time, audience retention, impressions, click-through rate, traffic sources);
  • basic YouTube channel and video information (titles, publish dates, durations).

The tool cannot upload, edit, delete or publish anything on your channel.

How we use it

The data is used only to produce internal reports that help improve future videos of the same channel (for example, finding the moment viewers stop watching).

Storage and sharing

  • Access tokens and reports are stored only on Tan Seven's own computers, protected by file permissions and a password manager.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with any third party, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. We delete the stored tokens and reports for a channel on request.
