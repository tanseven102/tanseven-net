What the app does for you

Five steps, fully automatic.

You choose the topic, language and length. The app does the rest; when it hits 100% your video is ready.

Find & download

Searches trending keywords for the topic, or paste links, or drop files into a folder.

Cut & score

Splits scenes, AI watches and scores the best moments, drops risky scenes.

Write narration

Written in your chosen language, voiced with T-GIỌNG ĐỌC, timed to each scene.

Edit the video

Best scenes first, opening hook, blur, logo removal, your channel logo.

Make a thumbnail

Grabs the strongest frame as the thumbnail, saved next to the video file.
