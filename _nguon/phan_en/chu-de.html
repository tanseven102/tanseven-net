6 ready-made topics

Pick a niche, the app knows what to look for.

Each topic has its own search keywords, scene-scoring rules and narration style. Switch topics with the "Topic" box at the top of the app.

🚗

Car crashes · narrated

Dashcams, collisions, narrow escapes. Narration explains what happened and the driving lesson.

🌪

Natural disasters · narrated

Flash floods, storms, tornadoes, earthquakes, volcanoes. Told like an on-the-scene report.

✈

Planes & ships · narrated

Crosswind landings, ships rolling in storms, breathless moments in the air and at sea.

📹

Security cameras · narrated

Strange, lucky and unbelievable moments caught on camera.

🐻

Wildlife · narrated

Hunts, standoffs, animal rescues. Told like a nature documentary.

😂

Funny · memes + sound effects

Funny compilations with a 30-second hook, memes, stickers and sound effects right where it's funny.
