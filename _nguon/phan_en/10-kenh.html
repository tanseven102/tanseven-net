What one button press does

01

Pick a topic + a language

For example "Car crashes" and "Español". Choose the voice and reading speed.

02

Press "Add 10 videos"

Each row is one video with its own length and output folder. Set one folder per channel and the work is split.

03

Press MAKE VIDEO

The app makes each video to 100%, thumbnail included. Switch topic or language and press again.

This is an example of how to organize your work, not an income promise. Results depend on your niche, how you publish, and YouTube's policies.
