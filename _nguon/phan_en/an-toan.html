Channel safety

Filter out what gets channels strikes, before editing.

The more countries you publish in, the cleaner your channels need to be. The app filters in several layers before assembling a video.

🔇

Mute source audio

Drops the original clip audio entirely, keeping only narration and transition sounds: no copyrighted music hiding in clips. One checkbox.

🌫

Auto-blur, drop graphic scenes

Injured people are blurred and overly violent scenes are cut, following YouTube's content rules.

🎬

Block movies & CGI

Hollywood movie scenes, trailers and CGI are rejected at search time and never reach your video.

🚫

Avoid claim-happy rights holders

Videos from agencies that claim aggressively (ViralHog, Jukin, Storyful, Newsflare…) are skipped by source name.

🧽

Remove other channels' logos

Detects and covers logos and watermark text in source clips, adds your channel logo.

🌑

Drop dark and broken scenes

Measures each scene's brightness; pitch-black or blank scenes are removed automatically.

The app filters out most common risks, but you remain responsible for your source clips and how you publish. Review each video before making it public.
