Questions you may have

Do I need a PayPal account to give?

In most countries, no. On the PayPal page you can choose to pay with a debit or credit card instead of signing in.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

No. is an independent YouTube channel, not a registered charity or church, so we cannot issue tax receipts. Your gift is a voluntary contribution to help us create these videos.

Is my payment safe?

Yes. Your payment is handled entirely by PayPal. We never see or store your card or bank details.

Will the videos stay free if I do not give?

Always. Every lesson is free for everyone, whether or not you ever give. You are welcome here either way.

Should I give here instead of to my church?

Please support your local church first. A gift here is something extra, only if you are able and wish to.
