2. How we use it

Order data is used only to match your bank transfer, issue your license KEY, provide support, and pay affiliate commissions when an order came through a referral link. Channel analysis data is used only to answer your request.

3. Third parties

  • YouTube Data API (Google) — phan_tich_kenh reads public channel and video statistics through the official API. Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
  • SePay / your bank — when you pay, the bank transfer notification (amount, transfer note, transaction id) reaches us through SePay to confirm the order.
  • Vultr — hosting provider of the server (Singapore).

We do not sell, rent or share data for advertising.

4. Retention

Order records are kept for as long as the license is active and up to 5 years for accounting. Server logs: up to 30 days. Analysis cache: up to 12 hours.

5. Your choices

You can use every read-only tool without giving any personal data. To access or delete an order record, email tanseven5@gmail.com with the order code; we answer within 7 days. Deleting an order record also ends support for that license.

6. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Signing keys and API keys stay on the server and are never sent to the AI or the plugin.

7. Changes

We will update this page and its date when the policy changes.
