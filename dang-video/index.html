Internal tool · Tan Seven

Upload finished videos to our own YouTube channels — privately, with the right metadata.

Tan Seven Publisher (Vietnamese name T-ĐĂNG VIDEO) is a desktop tool used by the Tan Seven team to upload videos we produce to the YouTube channels that Tan Seven owns and manages. Every upload is private by default; a person reviews it in YouTube Studio before anything becomes public.

The upload queue: each finished video shows its channel, prepared metadata and thumbnail before it is uploaded.

What the app does

1 · Pick a finished video

Our production apps write each video with a matching title, description, tags and thumbnail file. The app lists them per channel.

2 · Check before upload

It checks length, thumbnail and channel rules, and shows a full preview. Videos that break a rule are blocked.

3 · Upload as private

It uploads the video file to the selected channel with privacyStatus=private, sets the thumbnail and declares AI-generated content where applicable.

4 · Human review

The app returns the YouTube Studio link. A team member reviews the video and decides whether to publish it.

How we use Google user data

The app signs in with Google only for YouTube channels owned by Tan Seven. It requests:

ScopeWhy we need it
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.uploadTo upload our own video files to our own channel (videos.insert) and set their custom thumbnails (thumbnails.set).
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonlyTo read the name and ID of the channel that was just authorized (channels.list mine=true), so the upload goes to the correct channel.

The app never deletes, edits or comments on content, never reads viewers' data, and never shares Google user data with anyone. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details: Privacy Policy.

Who can use it

The tool is not offered to the public. It runs on Tan Seven's own computers and is used only by the Tan Seven team for channels the company owns. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and our Terms of Service.
