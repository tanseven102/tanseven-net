Desktop app for YouTube creators · by Tan Seven

Upload your finished videos to your own YouTube channel — privately, with the right metadata.

Tan Seven Publisher (Vietnamese name T-ĐĂNG VIDEO) is a Windows desktop app that uploads videos a creator has produced to the creator's own YouTube channel. It is used today by the Tan Seven studio for its own channels and is being prepared as a paid app for other YouTube creators. Every upload is private by default; the creator reviews it in YouTube Studio before anything becomes public.

Uses YouTube API Services. Tan Seven Publisher uploads videos through the YouTube Data API. See our Privacy Policy, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

The upload queue: each finished video shows its channel, prepared metadata and thumbnail before it is uploaded.

What the app does

1 · Pick a finished video

The creator's production tools write each video with a matching title, description, tags and thumbnail file. The app lists them per channel.

2 · Check before upload

It checks length, thumbnail and channel rules, and shows a full preview. Videos that break a rule are blocked.

3 · Upload as private

It uploads the video file to the selected channel with privacyStatus=private, sets the thumbnail and declares AI-generated content where applicable.

4 · Human review

The app returns the YouTube Studio link. The creator reviews the video and decides whether to publish it.

How we use Google user data

Each user signs in with Google and connects only the YouTube channel(s) they own or manage. The app requests:

ScopeWhy we need it
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.uploadTo upload the user's own video files to the user's own channel (videos.insert) and set their custom thumbnails (thumbnails.set).
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonlyTo read the name and ID of the channel that was just authorized (channels.list mine=true), so the upload goes to the correct channel.

The app never deletes, edits or comments on content, never reads viewers' data, and never shares Google user data with anyone — Tan Seven does not receive it. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details: Privacy Policy.

Who can use it

The app runs on the user's own Windows computer. It is used today by Tan Seven for its own channels and will be offered to other YouTube creators as a paid desktop app; each user connects only their own channels, and their tokens and files never leave their computer. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and our Terms of Service.
